Deceptive cover · Underlying fear

How Fear Hides

Fear seldom says its own name. It borrows the name of something respectable. Each cover below can be real on its face. Underneath, something else may be running it. These pairs are examples, not a code.

Wise caution

“I’m only being careful. No need to get pulled in.”

Underlying fear

Fear of involvement

Getting close means being responsible, and being hurt. Luke 10:31–32

Not one-to-one

Covers and fears mix

One cover, many fears. Busyness can hide fear of stillness, but also fear of failing, or fear of being alone with grief.

One fear, many covers. Fear of being known can look like privacy, humor, independence, or being helpful.

So these pairs are a place to start looking, not a verdict.

Scripture

What it says to the fear

  • Fear has been hiding since the garden. Genesis 3:10
  • Perfect love drives out fear. 1 John 4:18
  • God has not given us a spirit of fear. 2 Timothy 1:7
  • Do not fear, for I am with you. Isaiah 41:10
