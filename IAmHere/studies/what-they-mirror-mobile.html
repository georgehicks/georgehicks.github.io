Truth · Grace · Power

What Thinkers, Feelers, Doers Mirror

Thinkers, feelers, and doers mirror omniscience, omnipresence, and omnipotence. Not a copy of God, and not three personality types wearing divine names. Two layers of one image.

God, without limit

Omniscience

God knows what is. Nothing true is outside that knowledge, and God does not lie. Psalm 139:1–4

God, without limit

Omnipresence

God is present in every place. Not a dot on a map, but attentive nearness: nothing stands outside God’s regard. It becomes personal when it is turned toward someone as favor. Psalm 139:7–10

God, without limit

Omnipotence

God can act. Nothing God wills to bring about fails for lack of power. This power is not bare force. It is bound to knowing and to favor.

The human aim

Truth

What is the case?

Good: Fidelity to reality, including unwelcome reality.

Counterfeit: Prosecution: accurate, and unloving.

The human aim

Grace

Who is regarded, and how?

Good: Being actually with. Favor toward what did not have to be favored.

Counterfeit: Indulgence: nearness with no anchor, favor without reality.

The human aim

Power

What can be brought about, and at what cost?

Good: Agency that makes an intention hold.

Counterfeit: Control: force that neither tells the truth nor spends itself for someone.

The instrument

Thinking

Takes in what is the case and sorts it.

The instrument

Feeling

Takes in what matters to persons and is moved by it. Regard and value, not merely emotion.

The instrument

Doing

Takes an intention and makes it hold in the world.

The grid

Any instrument, any aim

The image is the whole grid, not the diagonal. The mature form is a leading instrument given to all three aims. The distortion is an instrument serving only its favorite aim.

Instrument

Thinking

  • Truth. Discern what is real, including the hard case.
  • Grace. Understand mercy without sentimentalizing it.
  • Power. Judge what force can and cannot do.
Instrument

Feeling

  • Truth. Be moved by reality, including reality that does not flatter.
  • Grace. Be moved by persons as persons.
  • Power. Feel the weight of what action will cost.
Instrument

Doing

  • Truth. Act in line with what is true.
  • Grace. Make favor concrete.
  • Power. Make the change, so intention takes on weight.
When one serves one aim

Distortions

  • Thinking aimed only at truth: Cold knowledge.
  • Feeling aimed only at grace: Flood: empathy with no anchor.
  • Doing aimed only at power: Tyranny: action without listening.
Not what people expect

The same instrument, another aim

  • A feeler can be fiercely truth-oriented.
  • A thinker can be the one who enacts grace.
  • A doer can be the one who refuses false power.
The whole

Why all three

  • Drop grace and truth becomes a weapon.
  • Drop truth and grace becomes permission.
  • Drop power and both stay sentiment.

Omniscience, omnipresence, and omnipotence are the three unlimited originals. Truth, grace, and power are the aims by which a person reaches for them. Thinking, feeling, and doing are the instruments that carry the aim. Image-bearing is not becoming one of the three. It is one instrument held toward all three, because each one alone counterfeits what it reflects.
