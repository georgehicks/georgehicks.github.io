Spirit inside the soul

Inner and outer man

The outer man is the body. The inner man is the soul. Spirit is the God-breath in that life, alive to Him or not.

The inner life

Soul · psyche. Life, self, thoughts, feelings, will.

Spirit · pneuma. God-breath. Alive, managing, or far.

Heart · kardia. The center Scripture most often names.

Mind · nous. Understanding. Not the brain.

Conscience · the inner witness.

Outer

Body

Flesh, breath, gut, hands. The brain is included. Rest, gripping, or taken.

Inner

Soul

Open, narrowed, elsewhere, or blank. Still present when the spirit is far.

Within

Spirit

Not a second inner man. God-breath in the soul. Home is this life alive to God.

The spirit itself

Alive, managing, or fading

  • Alive. The Spirit himself bears witness with our spirit. Romans 8:16
  • Managing. Having begun by the Spirit, are you now being perfected by the flesh? Galatians 3:3
  • Fading. My spirit faints within me. Psalm 143:4
