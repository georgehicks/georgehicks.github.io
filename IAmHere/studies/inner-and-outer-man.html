The inner-life words

Inner man · eso anthropos. The person turned inward. Not a synonym for soul.

Soul · psyche, nephesh. Life, self, person. Thoughts, feelings, memory, will.

Spirit · pneuma, ruach. God-breath in that life. Alive to Him, managing, or far.

Heart · kardia, leb. The center the Scriptures most often name. Seat of will, treasure, and faith.

Mind · nous. Understanding, the grasp of what is true. Not the brain.

Conscience · suneidesis. The inner witness, accusing or confirming.

Outer

Body

  • Flesh, breath, gut, hands.
  • The brain is included.
  • Rest, gripping, or taken.
  • Fight, flight, freeze, collapse.
Inner

Soul

  • Thoughts, feelings, memory, will.
  • Open, narrowed, elsewhere, or blank.
  • The inner man. Not the brain.
  • Still present when the spirit is far.
Within

Spirit

  • God-breath in the inner life.
  • Alive. Receiving, not straining.
  • Managing. Strength may not be His.
  • Fading. Love is hard to hear.

Home

Spirit alive to God, inside a soul that is present and open, in a body free to be still or move. Received, not earned.

Scripture's own pairings

Spirit, Soul, and Body Together

Scripture does not always name all three. Here is how it puts them side by side, and how the spirit itself fares.

Spirit, soul, and body

All three

  • May your whole spirit and soul and body be kept blameless. 1 Thessalonians 5:23
Two together

Soul and body

  • My soul thirsts for you; my flesh faints for you. Psalm 63:1
  • My soul longs, yes, faints for the courts of the Lord; my heart and flesh sing for joy. Psalm 84:2
  • That you may be in good health, as it goes well with your soul. 3 John 2
  • Fear him who can destroy both soul and body in hell. Matthew 10:28
Two together

Spirit and body

  • Let us cleanse ourselves from every defilement of body and spirit. 2 Corinthians 7:1
  • The body apart from the spirit is dead. James 2:26
  • A crushed spirit dries up the bones. Proverbs 17:22
  • A man’s spirit will endure sickness, but a crushed spirit who can bear? Proverbs 18:14
Two together

Soul and spirit

  • My soul magnifies the Lord, and my spirit rejoices in God my Savior. Luke 1:46–47
  • My soul yearns for you in the night; my spirit within me earnestly seeks you. Isaiah 26:9
  • I will speak in the anguish of my spirit; I will complain in the bitterness of my soul. Job 7:11
  • Piercing to the division of soul and of spirit. Hebrews 4:12
The spirit itself

Alive, managing, or fading

  • Alive. The Spirit himself bears witness with our spirit. Romans 8:16
  • Managing. Having begun by the Spirit, are you now being perfected by the flesh? Galatians 3:3
  • Fading. My spirit faints within me. Psalm 143:4
