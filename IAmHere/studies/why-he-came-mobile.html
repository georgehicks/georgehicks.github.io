A working outline

Why He came, and what He is up to

The errand, in the lines Scripture gives. What He opened, and what He is still doing.

Sent

The Father’s will

  • Not His own will. John 6:38
  • To finish the work, and glorify the Father. John 17:4
Reveal

Make Him known

  • To make the Father known. John 1:18
  • To bear witness to the truth. John 18:37
  • To come as light. John 12:46
Save

Seek and save

  • To save His people from their sins. Matthew 1:21
  • Not to condemn, but to save. John 3:17
  • To seek and save the lost. Luke 19:10
Give His life

A way home

  • A ransom for many. Mark 10:45
  • To take away sin. John 1:29
  • To bring us to God. 1 Peter 3:18
Break

Captivity undone

  • To destroy the works of the devil. 1 John 3:8
  • To break the power of death. Hebrews 2:14
  • Life abundant. One flock. The Spirit sent. John 10:10; 16:7
Intention

Wanted, brought home

  • God so loved the world. John 3:16
  • Where He is, we may be also. John 14:3
  • Abide in Him, and He in us. John 15:4
Spirit and likeness

What He is after

  • Guide into truth. Glorify Jesus. John 16:13–14
  • Conformed to the Son. Romans 8:29
  • Fruit of the Spirit. Galatians 5:22–23
Still up to

Greater works

  • The works He does, and greater works. John 14:12
  • As the Father sent Him, so He sends us. John 20:21
  • His sheep hear His voice. John 10:27
