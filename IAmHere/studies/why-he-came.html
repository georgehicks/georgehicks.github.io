A working outline

Why He Came,
and What He Is Up To

The errand, in the lines Scripture gives. First coming. What He opened, and what He is still doing.

01 · Sent

The Father’s will

  • To do the Father’s will, not His own. John 6:38
  • To finish the work He was given, and so glorify the Father. John 17:4
02 · Reveal

Make Him known

  • To make the Father known. John 1:18
  • To bear witness to the truth. John 18:37
  • To come as light. John 12:46
03 · Fulfill

Law, promise, sons

  • Not to abolish the Law and the Prophets, but to fulfill them. Matthew 5:17
  • To redeem those under the Law, and give adoption. Galatians 4:4–5
04 · Kingdom

This is why He was sent

  • To preach the good news of the kingdom. Luke 4:43; Mark 1:38
  • Good news to the poor, liberty to the captives, sight to the blind, the Lord’s favor. Luke 4:18–19
05 · Save

Not to condemn. To seek and save.

  • To save His people from their sins. Matthew 1:21
  • Not to condemn the world, but that the world might be saved through Him. John 3:17
  • To call sinners, not the righteous. Mark 2:17
  • To seek and save the lost. Luke 19:10
06 · Give His life

Ransom, sin, a way home

  • Not to be served, but to serve, and to give His life as a ransom for many. Mark 10:45
  • To take away sin. John 1:29; 1 John 3:5
  • To make propitiation. Hebrews 2:17
  • To suffer once for sins, the righteous for the unrighteous, to bring us to God. 1 Peter 3:18
  • To redeem a people for His own, and reconcile us to God. Titus 2:14; 2 Corinthians 5:18–19
07 · Break

Captivity undone

  • To destroy the works of the devil. 1 John 3:8
  • To break the one who had the power of death. Hebrews 2:14
08 · Life, and a people

One flock. The Spirit sent.

  • That they may have life, and have it abundantly. John 10:10
  • Other sheep, one flock, one shepherd. John 10:16
  • To make peace, and one new people. Ephesians 2:14–16
  • To go, so the Helper would come. John 16:7
The aim

To bring us to God.

  • The righteous for the unrighteous. 1 Peter 3:18
The life

Life, and life abundant.

  • He came that they may have it. John 10:10

What He reveals · what He is still doing

God’s intentions,
and the life He is after

Wanted

Loved, and given the kingdom

  • God so loved the world that He gave His Son. John 3:16
  • The Father Himself loves us. John 16:27
  • It is the Father’s good pleasure to give the kingdom. Luke 12:32
Saved, not lost

None of His lost

  • Not to perish, but to have eternal life. John 3:16
  • Not sent to condemn, but that the world might be saved. John 3:17
  • Of all the Father gives Him, He should lose nothing. John 6:39
Brought home

Where He is, we may be

  • To bring us to God. 1 Peter 3:18
  • A place prepared, that where He is, we may be also. John 14:2–3
  • Father, I desire that they also be with Me where I am. John 17:24
Sons · free · one

Children, and one people

  • Adoption, and power to become children of God. Galatians 4:5; John 1:12
  • If the Son sets us free, we are free indeed. John 8:36
  • One flock. That they may be one. Peace, one new people. John 10:16; 17:21; Ephesians 2:14–16
Alive to Him

Rest, life, a shared home

  • Life, and life abundant. John 10:10
  • Come, and He will give rest. Matthew 11:28
  • Abide in Him, and He in us. John 15:4
  • The Father and the Son make their home with the one who loves Him. John 14:23
Kept, and finished

Held to the end

  • No one snatches them from His hand, or the Father’s. John 10:28–29
  • He who began the work will bring it to completion. Philippians 1:6
  • A new heaven and a new earth, God dwelling with His people. Revelation 21:1–3
The Spirit

What He wants

  • Given, and remaining with us. John 14:16–17
  • To teach, and bring His words to mind. John 14:26
  • To guide into truth, and glorify Jesus. John 16:13–14
  • Witness that we are children. Love poured out. Help in weakness. Romans 8:16; 5:5; 8:26
  • Sealed for the day of redemption. Power to witness. Ephesians 1:13–14; Acts 1:8
Transformed

What we look like

  • A new creation. A new heart and a new spirit. 2 Corinthians 5:17; Ezekiel 36:26
  • Conformed to the image of the Son. Romans 8:29
  • Changed into His likeness, from glory to glory. 2 Corinthians 3:18
  • Fruit: love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, self-control. Galatians 5:22–23
  • Mind renewed. A body made like His. Romans 12:2; Philippians 3:21
Still up to

Greater works, because He goes to the Father

  • Whoever believes will do the works He does, and greater works. John 14:12
  • As the Father sent Him, so He sends us. John 20:21
  • Ask in His name, and He will do it, that the Father be glorified. John 14:13–14
  • His sheep hear His voice, and follow. John 10:27
  • Authority over the power of the enemy. Luke 10:19
  • Signs accompanying those who believe. Mark 16:17–18
  • He ascended and gave gifts. Ephesians 4:8
  • The Spirit gives gifts to each, for the common good. 1 Corinthians 12:7–11
  • Power when the Spirit comes, and we are witnesses. Acts 1:8
