Deceptive cover · Underlying fear

How Fear Hides

Fear seldom says its own name. It borrows the name of something respectable. Each cover below is real on its face. Underneath, something else may be running it.

What Scripture says to the fear

  • Fear has been hiding since the garden. Genesis 3:10
  • Perfect love drives out fear. 1 John 4:18
  • God has not given us a spirit of fear. 2 Timothy 1:7
  • Do not fear, for I am with you. Isaiah 41:10
