The structure of the gift

Not quite

God created us, so we are not God. He gave us room to love and know Him, or to reject Him, and an unfinished world He called good.

The room

Free to join Him

He wanted us to work this world with Him, in that freedom. We have used the freedom to reject Him.

The mercy

Guardrails, hazards

Reality protects us from becoming self-righteous, self-absorbed, and trapped inside ourselves.

The rescue

Save, and grow

He gave a rescue. To save us, and to grow us up in Him.

Exact enough

What had to be met

  • Near enough for water to run. Far enough that it does not boil off.
  • Air thick enough to hold heat and water. Thin enough not to crush the surface.
  • A magnetic field, or the star’s wind strips the air.
  • A crust that moves, so carbon is buried and returned.
  • Land and sea. Oxygen enough to live, not enough to keep the world on fire.
  • A moon large enough to pull the tides and steady the tilt.
The cycles

They do not divide

  • Day, moon, and year do not divide one another.
  • The year is about 365.24 days. A moon-month is about 29.5.
  • That mismatch forced the calendar, then the watching: seasons, tides, weather.
  • The week is seven because it was given, not because the sky produced it.
Who He is

Not our drawing

  • Father, Son, and Spirit. Not the family picture we would have drawn.
  • One man, one people, one cross.
  • Scripture in human words. God speaking, and men still arguing.
Left open

So love can be love

  • Room for faith is room for doubt.
  • A love that cannot be refused is not love.
  • The fall left the gift crooked. The gift is still the gift.
  • The end is not yet.
Not one of us · Mark 9

Measured by His name

  • “We tried to stop him, because he was not following us.” Mark 9:38
  • “Do not stop him… For the one who is not against us is for us.” Mark 9:39–40
  • Jesus did not count what the man lacked, or whose circle he was in. He counted whose name he was working in.
  • Not how much is still off. How much is true, and in whose name.
Invitation

The room He left

A certainty that already knows cannot be met. We do not wait until the picture is clean. We bring the unfinished place to Jesus, and take a step toward Home.
