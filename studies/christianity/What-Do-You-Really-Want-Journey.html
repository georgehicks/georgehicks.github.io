BANDO JOURNEY

What Do You Really Want?

Jesus surfaces the true desire. Abiding in the Son both shapes the want and opens the way to receive it. Asking is the fruit of remaining in Him.

Click any reference for the full Berean text. Inside the text, gold underlined words open an inline card with the key Greek or Hebrew.

1

Jesus Asks the Question

He does not assume. He surfaces the real want so it can be named and brought to Him.

“What do you want?”John 1:38 — First words of Jesus in John. The disciples answer by asking where He abides.
“Do you want to get well?”John 5:6 — To the man at Bethesda after 38 years.
“What do you want Me to do for you?”Mark 10:51 — Bartimaeus: “let me see again.” He receives sight and follows.

The Contrast

Same question to those seeking seats of honor (Mark 10:35–40). They do not know what they are asking. One want leads to following the Son; the other does not.

2

How Asking Works

The promise is real. The condition is remaining in the Son so His words form the wish.

“If you remain in Me and My words remain in you, ask whatever you wish, and it will be done for you.”John 15:7
  • John 14:13–14; John 16:23–24 — Ask in My name… that your joy may be complete.
  • John 4:10 — “If you knew the gift of God… you would have asked Him, and He would have given you living water.”
  • Matt 7:7–11 — Ask, seek, knock. The Father gives good gifts.
  • Matt 6:33 — Seek first the kingdom and His righteousness.
  • Psalm 37:4 — Delight yourself in the LORD, and He will give you the desires of your heart.
  • James 4:2–3 — You do not have because you do not ask. You ask wrongly, to spend it on your passions.

The pattern: real want → bring it to the Son → abide so His words reshape it → ask in His name → receive and follow.

3

Come and See / Jump In

In a fallen world, desires are disordered by default. The Kingdom grows here as men learn to want what the Son gives and then receive it from Him.

  • Name the actual want (not the polished version).
  • Stay with Jesus long enough for His words to touch the want.
  • Ask, then do the next thing He gives — like Bartimaeus, follow on the road.
  • Abiding is the connected way of life with Father, Son, and Spirit.
Jesus is the way, the truth, and the life. Abiding in Him is remaining in the Vine so the life of the Vine flows.John 14:6 · John 15

Surprising Connections

Desire in Scripture points forward to Christ — the One who satisfies every true thirst and hunger. Click references to read the full texts.

Thirst & Living Water

Isa 55:1 “Come, everyone who thirsts…” → John 4:10, 14; John 7:37–38; Rev 22:17. The well and the wilderness thirst both point to the Son who is the water.

Bread That Satisfies

John 6:35 “I am the bread of life…” Echoes the manna and Isa 55’s warning against spending on what is not bread.

Water from the Rock

Ex 17 / Num 20 — the struck rock yields water. 1 Cor 10:4 “that Rock was Christ.” Desire answered by the struck Son.

Seeking Leads to Abiding

John 1:38–39 — the opening question ends with the disciples staying with Him. Desire is meant to become remaining.

Sure Mercies of David

Isa 55:3 fulfilled in the risen Christ (Acts 13:34). The invitation to come and drink is the invitation into the covenant kept in the Son.

The Bride’s Longing

Song of Songs — the soul seeking the Beloved. The heart’s deepest want is for the One who is the Bridegroom.

Awaken to more life through a transformational relationship with Jesus.
