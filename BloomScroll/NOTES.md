# Bloom Scroll — build notes (folder: BloomScroll, was Manna; storage keys still start "manna.")

"Enough for tonight." A bounded evening scroll that replaces doomscrolling: you choose how long, it eases from playful to still, and
it lands in breathing and the presence of God (hand-off to **Treasuring** and **AbidingSteps**). The name is provisional and lives in
`index.html`, `manifest.json`, `sw.js` and the wordmark. (Working title was "SuperScrolling".)

## Design stance (UX reasoning, so it can be adjusted later)
- **Low entry friction.** Home is one big "Scroll N minutes" button using your last settings; mood and category are optional.
- **Descent, not just a timer.** `engine.js` `PHASE_WEIGHT`: *spark* (0–38%: math, riddles, jokes, games, places) → *wisdom* (38–74%) → *stillness*
  (74–100%: Scripture, Lawrence, Bible places; math and games drop out). The screen warms and dims toward the end (`#dim`, strength in Settings).
- **A bounded feed.** Visible track + orb + minutes left. At time-up: "That's your time", one +3 minute extension, auto-lands after 45 s.
- **Landing.** Six breaths (4 in / 6 out) with names of Jesus on the inhale and "I am here"-type words on the exhale, then a verse for the night,
  anything you hearted tonight ("Carry into the night"), and buttons to `../Treasure/` and `../AbidingSteps/`, or just rest (black screen with the verse).
- **"What am I reaching for?"** (wired / numb / restless / lonely / hungry / heavy) re-weights the mix and picks the landing line. Skippable.
- No scores or streaks. The only record is "Landed N of the last 7 nights" on the home screen.
- Ignores Reduce Motion on purpose (house rule). No keyboard UI except "Add your own".

## Content and licences
- **Scripture:** Berean Standard Bible (public domain), ~134 passages, fetched from bible.helloao.org into `text.js` (generated; see below).
- **Long passages (public domain, Project Gutenberg):** Brother Lawrence, George MacDonald, Thomas à Kempis, Epictetus, Marcus Aurelius, Pascal, Chesterton.
- **Short attributed quotes:** Lewis, Chambers, Augustine, Tozer, Willard, ten Boom, Weil, Kierkegaard, Thoreau, Seneca, Pascal, Lawrence — kept short on purpose.
  All were written from memory; **check any you plan to share** against the source.
- **Threads:** my paraphrases of Eldredge, Lewis, Chambers; labelled "in his spirit, not a quote". Nothing is put in the mouth of Winship or Nemeh —
  use **Add your own** (pasted text stays on the phone and joins the Wisdom feed).
- **Chambers:** the standard *My Utmost for His Highest* is the 1935 edition and probably not yet public domain, so only short quotes ship.
- **Relationship cards:** inspired by the Gottman Institute's published research; original wording; not their card deck.
- **Photos:** hot-linked from Wikimedia Commons (CC BY / CC BY-SA / CC0 / PD); credit shown on each card. Offline or on error the card falls back to generated art.
- **Drawn art:** `art.js` — seeded layered landscapes, no files. Drop your own (e.g. Grok) pictures into `art/` and list them in `art/manifest.json`; Scripture cards use them.
- **Unverified facts:** science and philosophy cards are written from memory and hedged where unsure. Spot-check before relying on a number.

## Mental math
Seven skills (add, subtract, multiply, divide, to-a-round-number, percents/fractions, squares), each with levels. A fast correct answer raises the level
(streaks help), a wrong one lowers it a little, the weakest skill is chosen most often, and one problem in five is a level-up stretch. A card is a
three-problem sprint; a wrong answer shows the trick for that skill. Input is a number pad that auto-submits when the answer length is reached (Settings: tap-the-answer).

## Treasuring game and the breathing bloom (v13)
- `treasuring.js` is a DOM copy of the real Treasuring app's stage (cloud, Jesus disc, heart, one slow drop at a time, bowl-shaped dock), with **five buckets** instead of seven: Release, Thanks, Intercede, Hold, Lies. Same short prayers, same rules as Treasuring (Intercede only for a person; a person or a thanks is never a lie; Hold just says its line and keeps the drop). Lists, fears, lies and truths are copied from `../Treasure/content.js`; there is no score. Not wired to Treasuring's saved data.
- The landing breath is the AbidingSteps petal bloom (`.breath-bloom`, 8 petals that spiral out and in, cross and heart at full bloom, ring and embers), retimed to one 10-second breath (4s in, 6s out) so it stays in step with the words. It starts on the first in-breath (`.bgo`).

## Voice engine (v17–v18)
Kokoro runs in `voice-worker.js`. Two engines: **gpu** (WebGPU, fp16 if the adapter has `shader-f16` else fp32; ~165 / ~330 MB) and **cpu** (wasm q8, ~90 MB).
Measured on a desktop: cpu 0.7x real time, webgpu ~6.8x. The cpu voice cannot keep up with itself, which is why it pauses between chunks.
`MannaVoice.detect()` finds out whether a GPU adapter exists; the app defaults to gpu when it does, and the settings show an engine chip row.
Downloads are tracked per engine in `S.voiceDl`. If the gpu voice fails to load or generate (untested on iPhone), `gpuFailed()` switches to cpu and says so.
Audio must start inside a tap on iOS: `V.unlock()` runs on every click/touchend/pointerup/keydown. Auto-read loads the voice in the background when the feed opens.

## Games (v20)
Nine games, all opened through `gamekit.js` (`MannaKit.run`): an Easy / Normal / Hard picker first (last choice remembered in `manna.glevel`, bests in `manna.gbest`), then a strip above the board (level ▾, status, New game).
A game is `{ id, title, blurb, levels:{easy,normal,hard}, make(level, host, api) }`; `MannaGames.reg(def)` wraps one for the feed; each file pushes its own into `MannaGames.list`.
- `games.js`: Star Watch (power-ups, UFO, drag-relative control), Hold the Pass (3 towers, upgrade/sell, wave types, ×2 speed), Lights (hints via GF(2) solver, undo, par)
- `games-board.js`: Checkers (forced captures, multi-jump, alpha-beta AI: depth 1+random / 4 / time-limited 9), Minesweeper (hold to flag, chording, free mistake on easy), Sudoku (unique-solution generator in 10 ms slices, next puzzle prefetched, notes, hints)
- `games-cards.js`: Solitaire (Klondike, tap-to-send or drag; draw 1 / draw 1 / draw 3; undo and hints on the easier levels)
- `papertoss.js`: Paper Toss (office; slingshot throw; pedestal fans re-randomised every throw, placed on the likely arc; gusting / sweeping on harder levels)
- `treasuring.js`: level sets the fall time (24 / 17 / 11 s)
No game-over screens: it rests and begins again. Tested in the desktop pane only; rAF there is throttled, so physics was checked with a fake frame clock. iPhone untested.

## Hand it over, wind-down and game time (v22)
- `release.js` (`MannaRelease`): people / cares / gripping. Tap-first (suggestion chips, "type your own" is the last resort). Choose orbs, **Give to Jesus** and they fly up into the heart-and-cross light; grips use **hold to open your hand** (2.4 s, with a How-tight? Loose / Firm / Clenched choice). Hold an orb for its story (times handed over, beads, "He carried it" settles it, remove). 🕯 = **Over time** (30-day count, per-item beads, loosening label for grips, a "He carried these" list). Store `manna.release` (history capped at 60 per item, no streaks or scores). Verses come from `text.js` only.
- **Wind-down** (`windDown('sleep'|'break', mins)` in app.js): fade → breaths (6 for sleep, 2 for a break) → guided hand-over (`MannaRelease.guided`: People, Cares, Gripping for sleep; Gripping only for a break; each skippable; Settings → "At wind-down" can turn it off) → sleep: the night verse and a dark Goodnight screen; break: the sitting is paused and a quiet countdown waits (+5, "Back to it" resumes).
- **Game time**: a game opened from Home starts a countdown (the Home "How long" chips) shown in the game bar and in a Home card; it ends with the same time-up choices (Wind down for sleep / +3 minutes / Take a break). ☾ in the game bar and ⋯ in the scroll offer the same. Not saved across an app close.
- No links out of the app any more (the Treasuring and AbidingSteps buttons on the landing are gone).
