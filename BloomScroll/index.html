Evening dimming

The screen warms and dims toward the end of your time.

Math input
Voice
Reading aloud

Every card from the Bible, wisdom, discovery, relationships, philosophy and places has a recorded reading (voice: Adam). Nothing is made on your phone; each reading is a small file that downloads when you reach the card, then works offline.

At wind-down

Winding down for sleep or a break goes through the breaths, then (if you like) handing people, cares and whatever has hold of you to Jesus.

Game time

Whether games you start from Home run on a countdown (using the minutes you pick). With a countdown, when time is up you can still finish the game you are in, and you can switch the timer off from the ☾ menu while you play. A sitting in the scroll that you open a game from can be paused the same way.

Game music

A soft, endless soundtrack plays while you play, grows quieter as your time runs down, and fades when you leave. It is made on the phone, so nothing downloads. To use your own tracks (for example from Suno), put the files in the audio folder and list their names in audio/manifest.json. On an iPhone the ringer switch must be on.

Bible places photos
Your own pictures

Put images in the art folder next to this app and list them in art/manifest.json (for example ["a.jpg","b.jpg"]). Scripture cards will use them. Pictures made in Grok work well.
