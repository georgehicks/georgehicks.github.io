AbidingFlow has been replaced

AbidingSteps does everything this prototype was reaching for — Step, Ahead and Abide, with your data kept in sync across your devices.

This copy is switched off on purpose. It had its own cloud sync that mirrored every piece of saved data on this website, so it could overwrite other apps' data (including AbidingSteps) on a device. Rather than patch it, the app and its sync are retired and the code is kept only for reference.
